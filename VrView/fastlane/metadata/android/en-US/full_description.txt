VrView turns your phone and a simple VR headset into a personal cinema for the videos you already have. Choose a folder and it becomes a playlist: put the phone in the headset and watch, with no account and no ads.

VrView is free and fully unlocked. Every feature is included. If you enjoy it, there is an optional one-time "buy me a beer" button.

FOLDER PLAYLISTS
• Pick any folder, with or without subfolders, and play it in natural order
• Shuffle, repeat all or repeat one
• A library inside the headset, with thumbnails, filters and sorting, so you can change video without taking it off
• Picks up where you left off, video by video

THE FORMATS YOU ACTUALLY FILM IN
• Flat, 180°, 360°, fisheye, EAC cubemap, 3×2 cubemap and the embedded mesh of VR180 cameras
• Mono, side by side, top-bottom and swapped eyes
• Detected automatically from the file's VR metadata, tags in its name (such as _180_LR) or its shape
• A full format editor for cameras that don't follow the standard: coverage, lens angle, circle center and size
• Save a format as a profile and use it for a whole folder

MADE FOR PHONE HEADSETS
• Anti-drift head tracking, so the picture stays in front of you
• Scan your headset's QR code to load its lenses, or use the 3-step calibration wizard
• Lens distortion correction and adjustable lens separation
• Look at a button to press it, or use the headset's button, a Bluetooth gamepad or a keyboard
• Level the horizon of a crooked video with your head

MORE
• Bookmarks with thumbnails and names
• Audio track and subtitle selection, including .srt files next to the video
• Technical information: codec, resolution, frame rate and hardware or software decoding
• A screen view without a headset: drag to look around and pinch to zoom
• Appears in "Open with" in galleries and file managers
• Available in English, Spanish, French and German

YOUR VIDEOS STAY YOURS
VrView needs no storage permission: you grant access only to the folders you choose. There are no accounts, ads or analytics. Your settings are saved on your device, in a small vrview.json file inside each folder, so they survive a reinstall.

GOOD TO KNOW
VrView plays video files stored on your device; it includes no videos and no streaming service. Which resolutions play smoothly depends on your phone's video decoder. Head tracking needs a gyroscope; without one, drag with your finger to look around. Requires Android 8.0 or later.
