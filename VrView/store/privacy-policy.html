VrView Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 4, 2026 · Español · Français · Deutsch

VrView plays video files stored on your device. It has no accounts, no ads and no analytics, and the developer receives none of your data.

Your videos and settings. You grant access only to the folders you pick. Your videos are never uploaded. Playback positions, bookmarks and format settings are saved on your device, in a vrview.json file inside the folder or in the app's own storage.

Viewer QR codes. If you scan a viewer's QR code and it holds a short link, the app opens that link to find the real address, so the link's service sees an ordinary web request. Nothing else uses the internet except Google Play.

Buy me a beer. This optional payment is handled by Google Play under Google's Privacy Policy. The developer receives no card details, and the app stores no purchase data.

Backups. Android may include the app's settings in your own device backup.

Children. VrView does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.
